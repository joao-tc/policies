Privacy Policy — RubiX: Cube Companion

Last updated: July 30, 2026

RubiX is a Rubik's Cube learning app. This policy explains, plainly, what happens with your data — which, in short, is nothing leaves your device.

No data is collected

RubiX has no accounts, no sign-in, no backend server, and no analytics or third-party tracking of any kind. We do not collect, store, or have access to any personal data, usage data, or identifiers from your device.

Camera and photos

RubiX uses your device's camera to photograph the six faces of your physical cube, so it can read the sticker colors and build a solve guide. This processing — image analysis and color classification — happens entirely on your device using Apple's on-device machine learning frameworks. Your photos are never uploaded, transmitted, or sent to us or to any third party. Nothing about your cube ever leaves your phone.

Third parties

RubiX does not integrate any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics services. No data is shared with anyone, because none is collected.

Children's privacy

RubiX does not collect any data from any user, of any age, so there is nothing to disclose or protect with respect to children's data specifically.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of RubiX adds a feature that does involve data collection — this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will reflect it.
